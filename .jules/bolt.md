## 2024-04-24 - [Optimize Product Filtering]
**Learning:** Combining string lowercasing operations inside the inner loop of `filter` operations on large arrays can be a performance bottleneck due to redundant calculations. By extracting the invariant operations out of the loop and wrapping the operation in `useMemo`, rendering speed increases significantly. The string lowercase operations for optional properties should gracefully handle undefined properties without matching empty strings where a missing property signifies false.
**Action:** Extract invariant operations outside of `.map` or `.filter` loops whenever possible, and always apply `useMemo` for derived states like filtering and sorting on product catalogs. Be cautious about preserving exact optional property truthiness behavior.
